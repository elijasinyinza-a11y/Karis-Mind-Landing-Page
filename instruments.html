Three proprietary instruments drawn from 14 years of organisational practice in Zambia's water sector — published in Business Beyond the Strategic Plan (Elijah Sinyinza, 2022) and deployed by certified practitioners globally.

GOLD™

Leadership Assessment

GOLD stands for Growth-Oriented Leadership Development — the author's own framing in Business Beyond the Strategic Plan. It measures one thing: how growth-oriented a leader's development is, examined through four progressive capacities the author teaches as the path of leadership growth.

The author teaches these as a progression — a leader leads themselves first, then leads others, then leads strategy, and growth is what sustains all three. GOLD measures where a leader sits on that path, individually or as a team, and delivers an AI-generated report grounded in the GOLD scoring framework.

Personal Leadership

The leader's internal foundation — self-discovery, self-leadership, and the alignment of intentions, thoughts, words, and actions. The author insists no leadership of others is possible without it.

People Leadership

Leading other people — how the leader influences, raises, listens to, and lifts others toward their potential, moving from influence by expression to influence by impact.

Strategic Leadership

Taking a vision through the seven stages of strategic execution — the author's 7Cs of Leading Strategy — and securing employee buy-in for change. The most operational of the four capacities.

Growth Leadership

The leader's commitment to balanced, ongoing growth and their deliberate use of growth environments. The author treats this as the engine that sustains the other three capacities.

OCAM™

Organisational Culture Diagnostic

OCAM — the Organisational Culture Assessment Model — operationalises the cultural philosophy of Termites in the Guestroom. Its foundation is an eight-dimension "DNA of Organisational Culture": the conditions leaders must address to shape a positive workplace culture. It is a multi-respondent diagnostic — respondents answer anonymously, and results aggregate into a single organisational score per dimension.

Beyond the DNA Index, OCAM profiles the organisation's dominant cultural orientation, measures Positive Mental Presence as an engagement outcome, and locates the organisation on a six-stage maturity path. The practitioner receives an AI-generated report grounded in the scores — with recommendations organised across the three cultural fronts.

Strategy — 7Cs

Strategic Management Operating System

The 7Cs is not a one-off survey. It is a lifecycle operating system for strategy — seven stages that occur logically as an organisation conceives, builds, communicates, and controls its strategic direction over time.

Unlike conventional strategy frameworks that produce a document and stop, the 7Cs is designed to sustain the practitioner-client relationship through the full arc of strategic execution. Each stage has defined inputs, outputs, and facilitation questions. The practitioner moves the engagement forward one stage at a time as the work progresses.

Cascade

Cascade turns a strategic workplan into a structured tree of workstreams and tasks — each with an owner, a due date, and a status. Change a task to Done and the overall progress updates immediately.

Two entry paths: decompose directly from a Strategy engagement's outputs, or upload an existing workplan to start tracking execution straight away.

⊡

Meeting Toolkit

Five components that cover the full meeting lifecycle: Meeting Planner, Agenda Builder, Live Capture, Minutes & Notes, and Action Points. Plan the session before it happens, capture what matters during it, and distribute structured outputs after it.

Every meeting can be linked to a client and an instrument, so the full context is in one place.
