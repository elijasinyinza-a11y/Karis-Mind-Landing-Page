Instruments

The frameworks behind the platform

Three proprietary instruments drawn from 14 years of organisational practice in Zambia's water sector — published in Business Beyond the Strategic Plan (Elijah Sinyinza, 2022) and deployed by certified practitioners globally.

GOLD™

Leadership Assessment

GOLD™ measures leadership capability across four behavioural dimensions — individually and as a group. It gives practitioners a structured, evidence-based picture of how leaders think, learn, relate, and decide.

Designed for use at any level: from a single leader seeking self-awareness, to a full executive team preparing for a strategic shift. Results are delivered via an AI-generated report grounded in the GOLD™ scoring framework.

Goal Orientation

How clearly the leader sets direction and pursues outcomes with sustained focus. Measures the link between aspiration and disciplined execution.

Openness to Learning

The leader's capacity to receive feedback, update assumptions, and grow through experience — individually and from those they lead.

Leadership of Others

How effectively the leader builds, develops, and motivates those around them. Covers both direct leadership and the creation of leadership culture.

Decisiveness

The ability to make timely, considered decisions under conditions of ambiguity or pressure — and to own those decisions through to execution.

OCAM™

Organisational Culture Diagnostic

OCAM™ diagnoses culture across seven subscales, surfacing the patterns that shape how people actually behave at work — not how they say they do. It is designed for group deployment: respondents answer anonymously, and their results aggregate into a single organisational score per subscale.

The practitioner receives an AI-generated diagnostic report grounded in the scores. The report does not produce generic commentary — it interprets the specific combination of subscale scores for this organisation, in this moment.

Strategy — 7Cs

Strategic Management Operating System

The 7Cs is not a one-off survey. It is a lifecycle operating system for strategy — seven stages that occur logically as an organisation conceives, builds, communicates, and controls its strategic direction over time.

Unlike conventional strategy frameworks that produce a document and stop, the 7Cs is designed to sustain the practitioner-client relationship through the full arc of strategic execution. Each stage has defined inputs, outputs, and facilitation questions. The practitioner moves the engagement forward one stage at a time as the work progresses.

Built-in Tools

Cascade

Cascade turns a strategic workplan into a structured tree of workstreams and tasks — each with an owner, a due date, and a status. Change a task to Done and the overall progress updates immediately.

Two entry paths: decompose directly from a Strategy engagement's outputs, or upload an existing workplan to start tracking execution straight away.

⊡

Meeting Toolkit

Five components that cover the full meeting lifecycle: Meeting Planner, Agenda Builder, Live Capture, Minutes & Notes, and Action Points. Plan the session before it happens, capture what matters during it, and distribute structured outputs after it.

Every meeting can be linked to a client and an instrument, so the full context is in one place.
