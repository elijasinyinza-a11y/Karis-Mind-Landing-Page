The Book

Business Beyond the Strategic Plan

Published in 2022 by Divine Keys Publishing, this book is the source document for all three Karis Mind instruments. Written by Elijah Sinyinza — based on 14 years of practice in Zambia's water sector — it challenges the assumption that a strategic plan is sufficient for organisational transformation.

The book introduces the frameworks that became OCAM™, GOLD™, and the Strategy 7Cs: a systematic, practitioner-deployable approach to diagnosing and transforming how organisations actually function — not how their plans say they should.

The platform does not replace the book — it operationalises it. Practitioners trained in the frameworks can deploy them at scale; the workspace handles the structure so practitioners can focus on the work.

Frameworks

The IP that the platform is built on

These are the conceptual frameworks developed in Business Beyond the Strategic Plan and operationalised through Karis Mind. They are referenced consistently across the platform, the certification programmes, and the practitioner community.

OCAM™ Core

The Three-Front Framework

Organisations must fight on three fronts simultaneously: the external environment (what the world demands), the internal environment (how well people work together), and the strategic front (where the organisation is going). The OCAM™ diagnostic is designed to surface the health of each front through its seven subscales.

OCAM™ Diagnostic

The Termite Index

Named after the silent, structural damage termites cause before the problem is visible, the Termite Index is a diagnostic tool within the OCAM™ framework that identifies culture problems early — before they manifest as performance failures. A low-barrier entry point that practitioner candidates encounter first in the certification funnel.

OCAM™ Trajectory

The Upward Spiral

The aspirational trajectory for an organisation that engages seriously with OCAM™ over time. As subscale scores improve and culture strengthens, the organisation enters a self-reinforcing cycle of higher performance, stronger culture, and greater capacity for transformation. The practitioner's job is to catalyse and sustain the spiral.

GOLD™ Core

The Four Dimensions of Leadership

Goal Orientation, Openness to Learning, Leadership of Others, and Decisiveness — the four behavioural dimensions that the GOLD™ instrument measures. They are not competency categories; they are observable, practical expressions of how a leader actually operates, drawn from field practice rather than academic theory.

Strategy Core

The 7Cs Operating System

Conception, Consultation, Confirmation, Composition, Communication, Coordination, and Controlling — a strategic lifecycle that treats strategy not as a document but as a continuous, managed process. The 7Cs treats the seventh stage (Controlling) as equally important to the first — most organisations plan well and execute poorly because they never build the discipline of Controlling.

OCAM™ Source Text

Termites in the Guestroom

The companion publication to Business Beyond the Strategic Plan, which develops the OCAM™ diagnostic structure in detail. The four-part diagnostic structure — observe, assess, interpret, intervene — that practitioners follow during an OCAM™ engagement is drawn directly from this text. The Termite Index, Three-Front Framework, and Upward Spiral vocabulary all originate here.

Canonical vocabulary

Terms used precisely across the platform

These terms have specific meanings in the Karis Mind framework. They are used consistently across the platform, the certification programmes, and all practitioner-facing material. When in doubt, refer back to this page.

Seven OCAM™ subscales

Always in this order: Clarity, Cohesion, Commitment, Communication, Competence, Creativity, Character. Never abbreviated, never reordered.

Four GOLD™ dimensions

Goal Orientation, Openness to Learning, Leadership of Others, Decisiveness. Not "competencies" — dimensions. Not interchangeable with generic leadership frameworks.

Seven 7Cs stages

Conception, Consultation, Confirmation, Composition, Communication, Coordination, Controlling. The seventh stage is always Controlling — never "Consolidation."

Termite Index

The short-form OCAM™ diagnostic entry point. Not a substitute for the full diagnostic — a signal that something is worth investigating more deeply. Always capitalised.

Three-Front Framework

The conceptual model behind OCAM™: external environment, internal environment, strategic front. Always used as a framework name, not a generic description. Always capitalised.

Upward Spiral

The aspirational trajectory described in the OCAM™ framework — the self-reinforcing cycle of culture improvement and performance. Always capitalised, always singular.

Practitioner

A certified Karis Mind operator who deploys instruments on behalf of client organisations. Always distinct from an end user (someone taking a survey) or a client stakeholder (someone reading a report).

Engagement

A structured programme of work between a practitioner and a client organisation. An engagement may involve one or more instruments and spans multiple sessions. Not interchangeable with "project" or "assignment."
